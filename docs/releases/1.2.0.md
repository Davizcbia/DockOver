# DockOver 1.2.0

[简体中文](#简体中文) · [English](#english)

## 简体中文

解压 Windows x64 便携包后，运行 `DockOver.exe` 启动程序坞和菜单栏，或运行 `DockOver Desktop.exe` 启动桌面小组件和叠放。

- **窗口**像 macOS 一样分三种：双击标题栏或把窗口拖到屏幕顶端会填满菜单栏和程序坞之间的区域，保留圆角，再双击或拖开即恢复原尺寸；贴靠分屏保持普通窗口；最大化按钮进入真全屏，菜单栏和程序坞隐藏并让出空间。
- **程序坞**悬停应用图标可预览其窗口并点击切换。拖出的图标会跟随鼠标，松手即移除时变为半透明并显示「移除」。修复从分隔条上方移出时的抖动，开着程序坞时截图工具的窗口模式恢复可用。
- **HDR 与色深**：程序坞、启动台、控制中心、通知、菜单和音乐壁纸在 HDR 下以 scRGB 输出，SDR 下抖动输出，暗部渐变更平滑。
- **桌面**叠放可原地展开、自由摆放；关闭叠放时小组件默认靠右。日历和天气小组件恢复上下滚动。
- **启动台**文件夹上下居中，内容多时可以滚动。
- **DockDrop**默认在后台运行：关闭窗口后，已配对的手机仍可发送，水球会自动弹出；可在设置中关闭。设置窗口改为同心圆角，修复简体界面显示繁体字。
- **菜单栏**状态图标按视觉边缘等距排列，输入法图标更清晰；控制中心的蓝色统一为系统蓝，声音输出的选中项恢复圆形标记。通知叠放不再发黑。

真全屏、贴靠分屏和 DockDrop 后台重连尚未在多种应用、多屏和实体手机上完整验证。

## English

Extract the Windows x64 portable package. Start the Dock and menu bar with `DockOver.exe`, or desktop widgets and Stacks with `DockOver Desktop.exe`.

- **Windows** behave like macOS: double-click a title bar or drag a window to the top of the screen to fill the area between the menu bar and the Dock, keeping rounded corners; do it again or drag it away to restore its size. Snapped windows stay normal windows. The maximize button enters real full screen, hiding the menu bar and the Dock and freeing their space.
- **Dock** shows live window previews when you hover an app and switches on click. A dragged icon follows the pointer and turns translucent with a **Remove** label when letting go removes it. Fixes jitter when leaving above a separator, and Snipping Tool's window mode works with the Dock running.
- **HDR and colour depth**: the Dock, Launchpad, Control Center, notifications, menus and the music wallpaper output scRGB on HDR displays and dither on SDR, for smoother dark gradients.
- **Desktop** Stacks expand in place and can be placed freely; with Stacks off, widgets default to the right. Calendar and Weather widgets scroll again.
- **Launchpad** folders are centred and scroll when they hold many apps.
- **DockDrop** runs in the background by default: after you close it, remembered phones can still send and the water ball pops up. Turn it off in Settings. The Settings window has concentric rounded corners, and Simplified Chinese no longer shows Traditional text.
- **Menu bar** status icons are evenly spaced by their visible edges and the input badge is clearer. Control Center uses one system blue and marks the selected sound output with a disc. Notification stacks no longer turn dark.

Real full screen, snapped windows and DockDrop background reconnection still need broader testing across apps, multiple displays and physical phones.
